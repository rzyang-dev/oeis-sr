In the mathematical frameworktraditionally used in the exact sciences, laws of nature areusually represented not by explicit rules for evolution, butrather by abstract equations. And in general what suchequations do is to specify constraints that systems mustsatisfy. Sometimes these constraints just relate the state of asystem at one time to its state at a previous time. And in suchcases, the constraints can usually be converted into explicitevolution rules. But if the constraints relate different featuresof a system at one particular time, then they cannot beconverted into evolution rules.
